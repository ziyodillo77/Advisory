We use analytics and advertising tools only if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. These may include Google Analytics, Yandex Metrica and the Meta (Instagram/Facebook) Pixel. Your cookie choice is saved in your own browser so that we do not ask again on every visit.

When you contact us through WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram, the privacy policy of that platform also applies.

6. Legal basis and confidentiality

We process personal data in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
