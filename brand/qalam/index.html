Brand guide for Qalam Advisory (legal entity: Mirzamoydinov & Partners), Tashkent. Tax, audit, company formation and Tashkent International Financial Centre advisory for investors from the CIS, Central Asia and the Gulf.

The idea

The pen, and the dot it leaves

Qalam (قلم · Калам) means "pen" in Arabic, Persian and Uzbek. It is the tool of contracts, accounts and knowledge, and it is honoured across the Islamic world (Surah al-Qalam).

Below the nib sits the nuqta, the diamond-shaped dot a calligrapher's qalam makes. Calligraphers use the nuqta as the unit for measuring every letter. In our mark it stands for precision, the measure behind every tax position, audit opinion and structure we deliver. The eight-point star in the nib comes from the tilework of Samarkand and Bukhara.

Usage rules

  • Leave clear space around the logo at least the size of the nuqta diamond × 2.
  • Minimum width of the horizontal logo: 140 px on screen, 35 mm in print.
  • Use the navy version on light backgrounds and the gold/white version on dark ones.
  • Use the bilingual Arabic version for Gulf presentations and the Arabic site.
  • Don't recolour, stretch, rotate or add shadows to the logo.
  • Don't separate the nuqta from the nib; it is the heart of the mark.
  • Don't retype the name in another font. Always use the supplied files.
  • Don't place the logo on busy photos without a navy panel behind it.
