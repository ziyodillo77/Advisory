Brand guide for Qalam Advisory (legal entity: Mirzamoydinov & Partners), Tashkent. Tax, audit, company formation and Tashkent International Financial Centre advisory for investors from the CIS, Central Asia and the Gulf.

The idea

One stroke of the pen

Qalam (قلم · Калам) means "pen" in Arabic, Persian and Uzbek: the instrument of contracts, accounts and knowledge.

Our Q is drawn the way a real qalam writes, with the thick-and-thin contrast of a broad nib. Its tail is a single pen stroke that ends in the nuqta, the diamond-shaped dot calligraphers use as the unit for measuring every letter. The nuqta stands for precision: the measure behind every tax position, audit opinion and structure we deliver.

Usage rules

  • Clear space around the logo: at least the height of the "A" in QALAM on every side.
  • Minimum width of the primary logo: 120 px on screen, 30 mm in print.
  • Navy logo on light backgrounds; white-and-gold logo on navy or dark photos.
  • Use the seal as a secondary emblem, never as a replacement for the logo.
  • Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.
  • Don't remove the gold nuqta or change its colour.
  • Don't retype the name in a font. Always use the supplied files.
  • Don't use the seal below 120 px; the lettering becomes unreadable.
