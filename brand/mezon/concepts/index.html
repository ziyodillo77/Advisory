Mezon Advisory: Logo Concepts

Mezon (Мезон · ميزان) means balance, standard and measure. Four modern directions, each built on that meaning rather than a literal set of scales, and each shown in real use. Lettering is converted to shapes in every file. Domain mezonadvisory.com was available when checked on 12 Oct 2026.

A

Equilibrium

RecommendedTypeface: Outfit

A circle split into two exactly equal halves, navy and gold, resting on the line of balance. In the name, the mark becomes the O of MEZON, so the logo and the word are one.

B

Measure

Typeface: Sora

Five bars of measured heights form an M. It reads at once as a chart, a ruler and the letter. The gold point above marks the standard.

C

Girih

Typeface: Manrope

The eight-point star of Samarkand and Bukhara tilework, rebuilt from two interlocking squares (navy and gold) woven over and under, with a balance point at the centre.

D

Equal-E

Typeface: Outfit

A pure wordmark: the E is drawn as three equal bars with the middle one gold. That's the sign of equality and a fair standard, hidden in plain sight.
