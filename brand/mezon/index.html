Brand guide for Mezon Advisory (legal entity: Mirzamoydinov & Partners), Tashkent. Tax, audit, company formation and Tashkent International Financial Centre advisory for investors from the CIS, Central Asia and the Gulf.

The idea

Equilibrium

Mezon (Мезон · ميزان) means balance, standard and measure in Uzbek, from the Arabic mīzān.

The mark is a circle divided into two exactly equal halves resting on the line of balance: navy above, gold below. It stands for the two sides every engagement must hold in equilibrium: investor and state, ambition and compliance, profit and protection. In the name, the mark takes the place of the O, so the symbol and the word are one.

Usage rules

  • Clear space around the logo: at least the height of the circle on every side.
  • Minimum width of the logo: 110 px on screen, 28 mm in print.
  • Navy logo on light backgrounds; white-and-gold logo on navy or dark photos.
  • Keep the two halves exactly equal. Balance is the whole idea.
  • Don't recolour the halves, swap them, or rotate the mark.
  • Don't close the gap between the halves.
  • Don't retype MEZON with a normal O.
  • Don't add shadows, outlines or gradients.
