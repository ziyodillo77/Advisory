Uzbekistan offers multiple legal pathways for foreign investors, business owners and corporate directors to live and work in the country long term. Whether you are establishing an LLC, buying commercial property or managing a regional subsidiary, the immigration framework has been modernized to attract international capital and executive talent. This guide outlines the key visa and residency options available under Uzbek law as of November 2026.

1. Investment Visas for High-Value Investors

Investors who bring substantial capital into the Uzbek economy can apply for a special investment visa. This status removes the need for frequent visa renewals and standard work permit hurdles.

  • Financial threshold: The standard investment visa requires placing an investment of at least USD 1 million into business entities or investment projects in Uzbekistan.
  • Duration: The investment visa is typically issued for up to 10 years, allowing multiple entries and exits without additional consular approvals.
  • Family members: Spouses and dependent children of the primary investor are eligible for accompanying family visas for the same duration.
  • Application process: Applications are submitted through the Ministry of Investment, Industry and Trade (MIIT) or via official online portals, backed by proof of funds and investment deployment.

2. Work Visas and Permits for Company Directors

If you register a company in Uzbekistan, such as an LLC, and act as its director, you generally require a work visa (usually classified under category E-2) and a work permit, unless you hold permanent residency.

  • Work permit exemption for certain executives: Foreign founders or general directors of enterprises with foreign investments above certain thresholds may benefit from simplified hiring rules. Check our company registration guide for corporate setup requirements.
  • Quota system: Standard foreign employees are subject to annual regional work quotas set by the Ministry of Employment and Poverty Reduction, though high-skilled specialists and company directors often enjoy streamlined approvals.
  • Duration: Work visas are typically issued for one year and can be renewed annually as long as the Uzbek company remains active and tax-compliant.
  • Corporate bank accounts: Once your residency or visa status is established, you can finalize opening a corporate bank account in Uzbekistan to manage operational funds.

3. Residence Permits Through Real Estate Investment

Foreign nationals who purchase real estate in Uzbekistan can secure long-term residency rights if the property value meets specified statutory minimums.

The purchase must be completed through officially registered transactions involving newly built residential or commercial real estate. The residence permit is granted to the buyer, their spouse, and minor children.

4. Step-by-Step Immigration Process

  1. Establish your legal basis: Register your company, make your real estate purchase, or finalize your investment project documentation.
  2. Prepare supporting documents: Gather corporate charters, tax registration certificates, bank statements, valid passports, and apostilled police clearance certificates.
  3. Submit electronic applications: File your visa or permit application online via the my.gov.uz state services portal or through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs consular department.
  4. Consular endorsement: Collect your visa sticker or residency card upon approval at the designated internal affairs office or Uzbek embassy abroad.
