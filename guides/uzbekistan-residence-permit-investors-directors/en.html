• An investor visa (multiple-entry, up to 3 years) is available for an investment of at least 8,500 base calculation units, about UZS 3.74 billion, in an Uzbek company.
  • Buying a new-build property gives the right to a residence permit: USD 300,000 in Tashkent, USD 200,000 in six regional centres and USD 100,000 elsewhere, for citizens of 111 listed countries.
  • A newer route grants a 5-year residence permit for a USD 250,000 payment, but its procedure had not been published by October 2026.
  • A foreign director usually needs a work permit, with exceptions for the first 3 months of a foreign-investment company, IT Park residents and residence-permit holders.

Uzbekistan has several separate legal routes for foreigners who want to live in the country, invest or run a company there. They differ in cost, duration and what they allow you to do, and some were changed or added in 2025 and 2026. This guide summarises the rules as of November 2026. Immigration rules change often, so confirm the current requirements with the migration authorities or with us before you apply.

2. Investor visa

A foreigner who invests at least 8,500 base calculation units (about UZS 3.74 billion from 1 September 2026) in a stake in an Uzbek company, or in a new enterprise with foreign investment, can receive an investor visa. It is a multiple-entry visa for up to 3 years, it can be extended inside Uzbekistan at the company's request, and it allows the investor to work in their own company. Family members can receive visas for the same period. The base unit changes from time to time, so the amount in soums moves with it.

3. Residence permit through property

Citizens of the 111 countries listed in the relevant decree can obtain the right to a residence permit by buying a newly built home in Uzbekistan for at least:

LocationMinimum price
Tashkent city and Tashkent regionUSD 300,000
Samarkand, Bukhara, Namangan, Andijan, Fergana and Khorezm regionsUSD 200,000
Republic of Karakalpakstan and other regionsUSD 100,000

The steps are: sign the purchase contract, register the title, then apply in person to the migration and citizenship division of the internal affairs authorities. Cheaper purchases are allowed for foreigners in some cases, but they do not give a right to residence. Older guides quote USD 400,000 for Tashkent; that figure is out of date.

4. The USD 250,000 route

A 2025 presidential decree introduced a 5-year residence permit, under a simplified procedure, for foreigners who pay USD 250,000, plus USD 150,000 for each family member (spouse, child or parent). No property purchase is needed. As of October 2026 the rules on where and how to pay had not yet been published, so this route cannot be used in practice until they are. We are tracking it.

5. Visas linked to IT Park and TIFC

  • IT visa: founders, investors and IT specialists of IT Park residents can receive a multiple-entry visa for up to 3 years, renewable without leaving the country. See our IT Park guide.
  • TIFC visa: people connected to participants of the Tashkent International Financial Centre will be able to receive visas of up to 5 years for themselves, their spouse and children under 18. The procedure is set by the Centre's own acts.

6. Work permits for foreign directors

Owning an Uzbek company does not require a visa or a permit. Working in it does. As a rule, a foreign director employed by the company needs a confirmation of the right to work, after which a work visa is issued if the director's country is subject to visa requirements. The employment contract is usually fixed-term, often up to one year, and renewed together with the permit.

  • The director of an enterprise with foreign investment may work without a permit for up to 3 months from the company's registration, while the permit is arranged.
  • IT Park residents can hire a foreign director and specialists without work-permit confirmation.
  • A foreigner with a residence permit is employed like an Uzbek citizen, without a separate permit.

A foreign director also needs a personal identification number (PINFL) and an electronic signature to run the company and its bank account; see our guides on company registration and opening a corporate bank account.

7. Which route fits you?

  • You will run your own company: the investor visa, or a work permit through the company.
  • You run a software or IT business: IT Park residency with the IT visa.
  • You want to live in Uzbekistan with your family: the property route, or the USD 250,000 route once its procedure is published.
