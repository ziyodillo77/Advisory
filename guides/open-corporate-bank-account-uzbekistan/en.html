• Every registered company can open accounts in Uzbek soums and foreign currencies. Most foreign-owned companies open both.
  • The bank needs the company's charter, registration details, the director's appointment, passport and personal identification number (PINFL).
  • A representative can file the application with a notarised power of attorney translated into Uzbek, but many banks still want to identify the director in person or by video.
  • With a complete file, accounts are usually opened in 1 to 5 working days.

The bank account is the step that turns a registered company into a working one: without it you cannot pay in charter capital, pay salaries or receive money from clients. Banks in Uzbekistan apply strict know-your-customer and anti-money-laundering checks, and a foreign shareholder adds documents to the file. This guide explains how the process works in practice in 2026. If the company does not exist yet, start with our company registration guide.

1. Choosing a bank

The banking sector mixes large state banks with private and foreign-owned ones. What matters most for a foreign-owned company is the quality of international payments, the speed of onboarding and whether the bank has staff used to working with foreign clients.

  • State-owned banks, such as the National Bank of Uzbekistan (NBU) and Uzpromstroybank (SQB), have the widest branch networks and long correspondent relationships, and are often chosen for large projects.
  • Foreign-owned banks, such as Ipoteka Bank (part of Hungary's OTP Group) and Tenge Bank (part of Kazakhstan's Halyk Group), are a natural fit for groups that already bank with the parent group.
  • Private banks, such as Kapitalbank and Hamkorbank, are known for modern online banking and fast onboarding for smaller companies.

Ask two banks for their list of documents and their onboarding time before you decide. The requirements below are the common core; each bank adds its own forms.

2. Documents

Charter and founders' decisionAs registered in Uzbekistan

Director's passport and PINFLA foreigner obtains the PINFL in Uzbekistan; a foreign director also needs a residence registration and, as a rule, a work permit
Beneficial owner informationWho ultimately owns and controls the company, with passport copies
Foreign shareholder's documentsRegister extract and charter of a corporate shareholder, apostilled or legalised and translated
Bank questionnaireBusiness activity, expected turnover, main counterparties and countries

Two practical details save time: a local mobile number, because banks confirm logins and payments by SMS, and the company's electronic digital signature, which you also need for tax filing.

3. The process step by step

  1. Collect the documents above and the bank's own forms.
  2. Submit the application at the branch, through the bank's online service where available, or through a representative.
  3. Compliance review. The bank checks the owners, the source of funds, the planned activity and sanctions lists, and may ask follow-up questions.
  4. Identification of the director in person or, at banks that offer it, by video.
  5. Account opening in soums and foreign currency, with access to internet banking.
  6. Pay in the charter capital within the period set by law and the charter.

Banks can take up to 15 banking days under their internal rules, but with a complete file many accounts are opened within 1 to 5 working days.

4. Can it be done without travelling?

Partly. A representative in Tashkent can collect the documents, submit the application and sign the account agreement with a power of attorney that is notarised, apostilled or legalised, and translated into Uzbek. Whether the director must still appear depends on the bank: some insist on a personal visit, others accept identification by video call. Ask the bank before planning a trip, and combine the visit with obtaining the PINFL and the digital signature if a trip is needed.

5. Currency rules to know

  • Companies can hold foreign currency and buy or sell it at commercial banks at market rates.
  • Foreign trade contracts are registered in the government's electronic system for foreign trade operations, and the bank monitors that payments for exports and imports arrive within the deadlines set by the currency rules.
  • Dividends can be paid to foreign shareholders in foreign currency after the dividend tax is withheld. See our tax guide for foreign investors.

6. Mistakes that delay approval

  • A vague description of the business. Name your main clients, suppliers, countries and expected monthly turnover.
  • Incomplete ownership information, or a chain of companies without documents for each level.
  • Translations in which names are spelled differently from the passports.
  • A foreign director without a work permit or residence registration.
