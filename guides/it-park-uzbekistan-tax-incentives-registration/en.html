• Residents pay no profit tax, VAT or social tax until 1 January 2028. From 2028 to 2040 the benefits continue, except VAT, for companies with more than 50% export income.
  • Instead of taxes, residents pay IT Park a contribution of 1% of total income, which can rise to 2% or 3% if the minimum export share is not met.
  • Employees pay 7.5% income tax instead of 12%, and foreign specialists can be hired without work-permit confirmation.
  • The application is filed online at my.it-park.uz and decided within 15 business days.

IT Park is Uzbekistan's technology park for software and IT services, set up by Presidential Decree UP-5099 in 2017. Its residents work under a special tax regime instead of the general rules. For an export-oriented IT company the savings are large, but the regime has conditions that changed in 2024 to 2026. This guide explains the rules in force in October 2026.

1. Who can become a resident

  • Only legal entities registered in Uzbekistan. Individual entrepreneurs and the self-employed cannot become residents. A foreign company first opens an Uzbek LLC; see our company registration guide.
  • Permitted activities only. The company must carry out activities on the approved list: software development, IT services, data processing, IT education, business process outsourcing and similar. Income from activities outside the approved business plan is taxed under the general rules.
  • Excluded businesses. From 1 April 2026 the benefits do not apply to payment organisations, payment system operators, marketplaces and microfinance organisations.
  • A realistic business plan describing the services, their volume, how the company earns money and the planned results. A mismatch between the plan and the actual project is the most common reason for questions from IT Park.

Until 1 January 2028

Residents are exempt from all taxes and mandatory contributions, including VAT and social tax, on income from their approved activities.

From 1 January 2028 to 1 January 2040

Presidential Decree UP-157 of 14 October 2024 extends the tax and customs benefits, except VAT, to 1 January 2040. The extension applies only to companies whose export income is more than 50% of total income, and to IT education providers whose graduates mostly go on to work at exporting residents. A company that sells mainly to the Uzbek market should plan for the general regime after 2027.

Dividends and foreign suppliers

From 1 February 2025 to 1 January 2040, dividends paid to foreign founders are taxed at 5% instead of 10% if exports exceed 50% of the resident's income. Separately, foreign IT suppliers from countries without a tax treaty with Uzbekistan can be exempt from withholding tax on services to large exporting residents until 2030. Compare this with the general rules in our tax guide for foreign investors.

Customs

Equipment, components and software imported for the company's own use are exempt from customs duties (not customs fees) if they are not produced in Uzbekistan and an expert conclusion is obtained.

3. The contribution and the export share

Instead of the exempt taxes, residents pay IT Park a contribution of 1% of total monthly income, due by the 20th of the following month. The base is broad: it includes interest, exchange gains and other income, not only IT revenue. Large exporters pay less: 0.75% above USD 10 million of exports in 12 months, and 0.5% above USD 20 million.

Companies with annual income of 5 billion soums or more must meet a minimum export share: 20% from 1 April 2026 and 35% from 1 January 2027. If they do not, the contribution rises to 2%, or 3% for income of 100 billion soums or more. Companies below 5 billion soums of annual income are not subject to this test.

4. How to apply

  1. Register an LLC in Uzbekistan with IT activities in its charter.
  2. Prepare the business plan: services, volumes, revenue model, staff and export markets.
  3. Apply online through your account at my.it-park.uz. Only the application and business plan are required.
  4. Document check within 3 business days. If something is missing, you have 5 business days to correct it.
  5. Expert review within 10 business days, sometimes with a short presentation of the project.
  6. Decision within 15 business days of filing. Approved companies are entered in the register of residents within 3 business days and receive a certificate.

After approval, the company must keep its activities in line with the business plan and pay the contribution on time. IT Park removes residents that break the rules: more than 200 companies lost their status in 2024.

5. Hiring foreign specialists

  • Residents can hire foreign specialists without work-permit confirmation and without quotas.
  • Founders, investors and IT specialists of residents can receive an IT visa: multiple-entry, valid for up to 3 years and renewable without leaving Uzbekistan.
  • For a specialist, IT Park recommends the visa if the person earned at least USD 30,000 a year from IT work, has a diploma or certificate and a guarantee letter from the employer. The review takes up to 30 calendar days and is free.

6. Is IT Park the right choice?

IT Park works best for companies that export software or IT services and plan to keep exports above 50% of income: they keep the core benefits until 2040. Companies selling mainly in Uzbekistan still benefit until 2028, but should budget for VAT and for a higher contribution if their export share stays low. Financial services firms should look at the Tashkent International Financial Centre instead.
